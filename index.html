Browser Toy

Draw toys and let the stick figure play

Create quick doodles (swords, platforms, props) directly on the canvas. Use the arrow keys to move, space to jump, and watch the stick figure grab nearby swords to swing them around.

Left click / drag Draw a doodle for the selected item type
Arrow keys Walk the stick figure
Space Jump
R Reset doodles
Draw on the canvas, then walk the stick figure into your doodles.
